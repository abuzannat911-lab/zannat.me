To update the website automatically whenever you push to GitHub:

  1. Go to GitHub: abuzannat911-lab/zannat.bd > Settings > Webhooks > Add webhook
  2. Payload URL: https://your-domain.com/api/system/webhook
  3. Content type: application/json
  4. Select: Just the push event and click Add webhook.
